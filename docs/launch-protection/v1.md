# Mandatory launch protection v1

Status: implementation candidate. No deployed contract, signer activation or public availability is implied. Applies to new launches on Ethereum and Robinhood after explicit policy activation; existing pools are immutable.

## Fixed rules

- Starting fully diluted valuation is at least USD 30,000 using total supply and a fresh independent quote/USD price. Integer tick selection rounds upward in valuation; stale or missing USD evidence fails closed. Supply remains configurable.
- Every receiving wallet has a cumulative inflow budget of 2% of launch supply for the first 300 seconds, including the dev and exempt wallets. The hook also limits cumulative purchases by each authenticated buyer to 2%, so redirecting delivery cannot multiply one buyer’s allowance. Outgoing transfers, sells and burns never restore that budget. Minting after construction is absent. PoolManager is exempt only as settlement infrastructure; no ordinary wallet is exempt.
- The total opening buy fee starts at 99% and decreases linearly to the normal fee over 30 seconds. The additional opening component then becomes zero. With normal fees of 1%, the additional rate is 98% at launch and 49% after 15 seconds. Normal platform and project fees remain explicit and separate.
- Dev plus no more than three unique nonzero wallets are exempt from the additional opening fee only. These addresses are fixed before initialization, cannot be changed later, and never bypass the 2% limit.
- The additional opening fee accrues in a separate fully backed native fee vault, payable only to the platform treasury `0xD88539d3c4C460136a733A3Fd60cf6BF269079da`. Any caller may trigger the payout but cannot change its recipient. The normal 0.3% platform fee and project fee have separate accounting. The initializer’s base LP position is permanently locked; it has no withdrawal, fee collection, arbitrary call or upgrade path.
- No tx.origin or user-provided hookData establishes exemptions. During the first 300 seconds, buys accept only a runtime-pinned router exposing its authenticated initiator, plus the fixed initializer's atomic initial purchase.
- Sells remain possible without a new anti-sniper sell fee. Every real buy/sell and initial purchase still accrues the 0.3% platform fee once.

## Components

The candidate pairs a fixed-supply protected token, a read-only fee module for the sealed Native30 kernel, and an atomic initializer. Token enforcement covers transfers and PoolManager withdrawals; fee enforcement runs in the exact kernel. Initialization binds the token, pool, module, creator, oracle, opening price and locked liquidity. Custom project logic can use separately reviewed compatible modules; a declaration alone is never launch evidence.

## Proof and rollout

Before activation require exact source/compiler/runtime binding, fork tests of initial purchase and ordinary buys/sells, both asset orders where supported, all boundaries, spoofed router rejection, transfer/cumulative limit, whitelist mutation rejection, fixed-recipient fees and LP custody. The same policy constants and tests apply to both chains. Chain-specific oracle, router runtime, deployment and source verification remain separate evidence.

Website preview, API admission, reviewer approval and final permit issuance must consume the same versioned policy. No API key or reviewer may waive the protection with a flag. Existing unused approvals must be assessed under an explicit transition before a new policy is represented as mandatory. Already signed permits need separate onchain expiry/revocation treatment.

## Limits

This reduces early concentration and makes immediate entry expensive. It cannot prove one human per wallet, prevent wallet splitting or guarantee the absence of bots/MEV. USD 30,000 is a launch valuation, not USD 30,000 of redeemable collateral. A 1% notional allocation is USD 300 before curve price impact and fees, not a fixed execution price.

## Price-source binding

Native quotes use fixed Chainlink ETH/USD proxies: Ethereum `0x5f4eC3Df9cbd43714FE2740f5E3616155c5b8419` with a 3,600-second heartbeat, and Robinhood `0x78F3556b67E17Df817D51Ef5a990cDaF09E8d3A9` with an 86,400-second heartbeat. The age limit is fixed per chain; applicants cannot supply another feed or widen the tolerance. The observed runtime hash is pinned at deployment. Nonpositive, future, incomplete or expired rounds reject initialization. This valuation is based on the latest valid oracle round, not a promise about immediate external market prices.
